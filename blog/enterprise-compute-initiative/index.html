Enterprise Compute Initiative (ECI): a guide for Singapore companies

Last updated: 28 October 2026

BizGrants Consulting · 28 October 2026 · 5 min read

The short answer: the Enterprise Compute Initiative (ECI) is a S$150 million programme from Budget 2025, run by Digital Industry Singapore (DISG), that pairs companies with participating cloud service providers for the AI tools, compute and consultancy needed to build AI into their business. DISG funds 70% of consultancy costs, capped at S$105,000 per company. It is aimed at companies with at least 10 staff in Singapore, a tech team of at least two and backing from the CEO, so it suits a different stage of AI maturity from most SME grants.

This guide covers what the ECI funds, who can apply, how to apply, and how it compares with WDG(JR+) and the EDGE Grant. All funding is subject to eligibility and approval.

What the ECI funds

The ECI combines three things through the participating cloud service providers: access to AI tools, compute to run them, and consultancy to design and build the use case. DISG’s consultancy support covers 70% of actual costs, capped at S$105,000 per company, so the company pays the remaining 30%.

The use case must use AI for at least one of these objectives:

  • Process improvement
  • New product development
  • Research and development
  • Contribution to the broader ecosystem

DISG also expects the use case to show impact for the company or its industry and to lead to the company setting up its own AI team. That last point is what separates the ECI from most SME support: it is built to grow in-house AI capability, not only to deliver one project.

Who can apply, and who it does not suit

DISG’s baseline criteria are a business entity registered or incorporated and physically present in Singapore, sponsorship at CEO or equivalent level, at least 10 staff based in Singapore, and a tech team of at least two people. Companies from all sectors may apply except gambling and betting.

Those criteria leave out many smaller SMEs. If you have fewer than 10 staff in Singapore, or no in-house tech team, the more practical routes are usually:

  • WDG(JR+) for a custom AI workflow that changes how people work, funded at 70% for SMEs together with job redesign, with the build done by an implementation partner.
  • The EDGE Grant for listed digital solutions and other activities.

How to apply

  1. Define the use case. Describe your AI ambition, the objective it serves and the impact you expect, in a way that leads to an in-house AI team.
  2. Prepare the documents. Your Corppass login and your latest audited financial statements.
  3. Choose a cloud service provider. You can state a preferred provider, and you are free to choose any of the participating ones, not only the one you already use.
  4. Submit through DISG. Applicants are generally told the outcome within 4 weeks.
  5. Onboard. If successful, the assigned cloud service provider contacts you with onboarding information, including the programme milestones.

One point to confirm with DISG before you start: an older DISG FAQ says companies need to work with a pre-qualified consulting partner on the ECI list. Check whether that still applies to your application.

Combining the ECI with other support

The rule is the same as for every Singapore scheme: each cost can only be funded once. A company could, for example, use the ECI for AI consultancy on a cloud platform, while the redesign of the roles affected by the new system is scoped separately under WDG(JR+), and staff moving into those redesigned roles are supported under the Career Conversion Programme. Keep a cost register that shows which scheme funds each line, and check with each agency before relying on a combination.

What to check before you apply

  • The application window. Check DISG’s programme page for whether applications are open and any closing date.
  • The consulting partner requirement, as above.
  • What the cloud provider contributes, such as credits or tools, and on what terms.
  • Your own share. The consultancy support covers 70%, so budget for the remaining 30% and for costs outside the consultancy.

Frequently asked questions

  • Q: What is the Enterprise Compute Initiative?
    A: A S$150 million programme from Budget 2025, run by Digital Industry Singapore, that pairs companies with participating cloud service providers for AI tools, compute and consultancy to build AI into their business.
  • Q: How much does the ECI fund?
    A: DISG funds 70% of consultancy costs, capped at S$105,000 per company, on a total supportable consultancy cost of S$150,000. The company pays the remaining 30%.
  • Q: Who is eligible for the ECI?
    A: A business entity registered or incorporated and physically present in Singapore, with CEO or equivalent sponsorship, at least 10 staff based in Singapore and a tech team of at least two. All sectors may apply except gambling and betting.
  • Q: How do I apply for the ECI?
    A: Apply online through DISG with your Corppass login and latest audited financial statements, and describe your AI use case. You can state a preferred cloud service provider. Applicants are generally told the outcome within 4 weeks.
  • Q: Can a small SME use the ECI?
    A: Only if it meets the criteria, including at least 10 staff in Singapore and a tech team of at least two. Smaller SMEs usually find WDG(JR+) or the EDGE Grant a better fit for an AI project.
  • Q: Can I combine the ECI with WDG(JR+)?
    A: On different costs, possibly, but the same cost cannot be funded twice. For example, the job redesign around a new system can be scoped separately from the ECI consultancy. Check with each agency before relying on a combination.
