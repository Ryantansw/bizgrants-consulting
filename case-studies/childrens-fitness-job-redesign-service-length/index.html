Job redesign eligibility check for a digital marketing role at a fitness franchise

Case / 2025−48 Children’s fitness franchise Job redesign · eligibility checked early

A children’s fitness franchise wanted to move an existing employee from franchise development into a full-time digital marketing lead role through job redesign. The redesign itself was sound. The employee had been part-time until the month the new role began, so the role did not qualify for job redesign at that point.

Industry context

Franchise businesses often grow a digital marketing function out of whoever knows the brand best. Redesigning an existing role is usually the right instinct, and job redesign support exists for it, but it comes with service requirements that part-time arrangements do not meet.

What was meant to change

Franchise recruitment and expansion work was to be removed. Social channels, content calendars and agency coordination were to be added, as a full-time role.

Our strategy

We reviewed the employee’s employment records, contract, payroll contribution history and salary against the pathway, then asked the assessor for a pre-check before any application was prepared. Job redesign requires twelve months of full-time service. Part-time service does not count towards the job redesign requirement, so the role did not qualify at that point. The assessor confirmed this, and the client had a clear answer within about three weeks of the first call.

What the engagement covered

  • Employment records, contract, payroll contribution history and salary reviewed against the pathway.
  • Service length checked against the job redesign requirement.
  • Assessor pre-check obtained before any application was drafted.

Outcome

Role
Part-time franchise development lead to full-time digital marketing lead
Pathway
Job redesign reskilling, digital marketing
Strategic value
A clear answer for the employer within about three weeks

Key takeaway

Check service history before designing the role. Part-time service does not count towards the job redesign requirement.
