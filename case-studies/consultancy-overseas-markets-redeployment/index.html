CCP approved for a director posted overseas by a market-entry consultancy

Case / 2026−45 Marketing and market-entry consultancy Overseas markets · job redesign

A small market-entry consultancy wanted its Asia client services director to spend six months in Australia, building business on the ground instead of managing accounts from a distance. The overseas markets pathway supports exactly that, and the application was approved.

Industry context

Market-entry consultancies sell local knowledge. A director who has originated deals and built partnerships in a market is worth more to clients than one who has managed that market remotely. The overseas markets pathway exists to support that kind of in-market experience for Singapore-based staff.

What changed and why CCP applied

The role moved from servicing remote accounts across Asia to originating deals and partnerships in Australia, with AI-assisted market analysis and proposal work built in. It was redesigned as a regional business development director role for the posting.

Our strategy

The plan was built around a single in-market deployment. The application was made by the Singapore employer, with the posting set out in the employer’s expatriation agreement. It was approved.

OJT activities included

  • Prospect meetings and deal origination in the target market.
  • Building local partnerships with firms in the market.
  • AI-assisted market analysis for client engagements.
  • Drafting client proposals with generative AI tools.

Outcome

Role
Client services director, Asia, redeployed as regional business development director in Australia
Pathway
Overseas markets pathway, job redesign
Status
Approved
Strategic value
In-market experience for a director whose clients need it
