Checking the whitelist before making the promise

Case / 2026−27 Food services / restaurant group Whitelist-governed pathway · revenue management

A restaurant and cafe group sought conversion support for one hire. The relevant food services pathway funds only a defined list of supported job roles, and the assessor also had to be satisfied that the role was genuinely different from the candidate’s past work. Both conditions had to be met at once.

Industry context

Food services operates on thin margins and high turnover, and the sector’s conversion pathway is correspondingly prescriptive: a published list of supported job roles, with limited room to argue that a role not on the list should count. That prescriptiveness is a constraint, but it is also a gift, because it makes the eligibility question answerable on day one.

What changed and why CCP applied

The candidate came from food wholesale and manufacturing, not hospitality. Marketing and customer retention sat too close to that background. Revenue management, as a discipline rather than a function of the reservation book, did not, and it was capability the group did not have.

Our strategy

The supported-roles list was checked first. In our consultation process, we then aligned the role scope with a funded position that was also clearly distinct from the candidate’s past work, which pointed to revenue management. Because food services applications frequently turn on demonstrating that the candidate’s previous position was genuinely different, prior salary and prior role records were verified to a higher standard than usual.

OJT activities included

  • Supported job role list checked against the position before the scope was finalised.
  • Role scope aligned with a funded position that was distinct from the candidate’s previous work.
  • Prior role and remuneration records verified to confirm a genuine conversion.
  • Offer letters and payroll records reconciled against the application position.
  • Training plan refined at the agency’s request during review.

Outcome

Role
Revenue management
Pathway
Sector-specific food services conversion pathway
Application status
Submitted; the agency indicated general support and asked for a refined training plan
Strategic value
A role the group did not have, scoped to a funded position and to a genuine conversion

Key takeaway

Some programmes run on a fixed whitelist, and the assessor still tests the role against the candidate’s past. A title can be on the list and still be too close to what the person did before. Checking both early saves a client from building a hire around an assumption.
