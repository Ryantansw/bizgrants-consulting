12

Advanced manufacturing

Automation, robotics, IoT and manufacturing engineering roles across the precision engineering ecosystem.

Eligible staffNew and existing
Min. salary$2,500
Support duration6 months (new), 3 months (existing)
Common job families, example titles and use cases

Common job families

Automation and robotics engineering IoT and digital applications Manufacturing and process engineering Equipment and maintenance engineering Production planning and supervision Quality assurance and control Product development and design

Example job titles

  • Automation engineer / assistant engineer
  • Robotics engineer
  • IoT application engineer
  • Manufacturing / production engineer
  • Process engineer / equipment engineer
  • Product development engineer
  • Quality assurance / quality control engineer

Typical conversion use cases

  • Reskilling manual welders into robotic welding programming, jig design and system maintenance
  • Smart-factory digitalisation that moves staff from manual data entry to production analytics and predictive maintenance
  • Service engineers converting into digital application roles covering IoT, cloud monitoring and data-driven troubleshooting
  • Mid-career hires moving from research or adjacent industries into hands-on manufacturing engineering roles

Scoping notes

  • New hires: role substantially different from the previous job, permanent or 12-month-plus contract, programme starts within 3 months of employment
  • Existing staff: employed more than 1 year, supported by a business transformation and job redesign plan
  • Structured OJT plan is validated and endorsed before commencement
  • Open to any Singapore-registered employer adopting automation or advanced manufacturing, not only automation vendors
