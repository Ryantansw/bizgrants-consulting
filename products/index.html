HuntSales

An AI-driven B2B outreach CRM built in Singapore for sales teams across Asia-Pacific.

CalendarSync

Real-time calendar sync and scheduling links for people who juggle more than one calendar.

  • Two-way sync across Google Calendar and Outlook, plus Apple Calendar (iCloud)
  • Blocker mode shares busy times only; Mirror mode copies event details
  • Scheduling links that only offer times when you are free across all your calendars

02 · Why we build them

The work that comes after the funding.

Our grants work puts us inside Singapore SMEs’ hiring, sales and admin every week. Once a role is funded or a team is redesigned, the next questions are practical: where do the buyers come from, and how do we get the first meeting booked? HuntSales and CalendarSync answer those two questions.

If you are redesigning sales or admin work around AI, our AI transformation service maps the workflow and redesigns the roles around it, and the AI use case library shows 47 examples by function and industry. All funding is subject to eligibility and approval.

03 · Common questions

About our products.

Are HuntSales and CalendarSync part of BizGrants Consulting?

Yes. HuntSales and CalendarSync are brands of BizGrants Consulting Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore.

What does HuntSales do?

HuntSales is an AI-driven B2B outreach CRM built in Singapore. It finds buyers across Singapore and Asia-Pacific and reaches them by email and phone from one platform, or its telemarketers make the calls for you.

What does CalendarSync do?

CalendarSync syncs Google Calendar and Outlook in real time, plus Apple Calendar, so you are not double-booked. Its scheduling links only offer times when you are free across all your calendars. The booking on this website runs on it.

Do I need HuntSales or CalendarSync to work with BizGrants?

No. They are separate products. Our grants advisory does not require either of them.
