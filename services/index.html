Pick the route by what you are changing.

  • Hiring someone new?Tap on CCP.

  • Doing more than one?The routes work together: redesign the roles and implement the AI under WDG(JR+), then apply for CCP salary support for the staff whose roles change.
02 · Three pillars

Three pillars, one partner.

Pillar 01

Career Conversion Programme (CCP)

Salary support for new hires and existing staff.

  • 70% of monthly salary, or 90% for staff aged 40 and above or long-term unemployed
  • New hires and existing staff, across the Programme Pathways
  • Pathway checks, applications and claims handled end to end
Explore CCP →Programme Pathways →
Pillar 02

WDG(JR+) job redesign

Redesign jobs with 70% funding. Redesigned staff can then be invited to apply for CCP salary support.

  • 70% for SMEs and 50% for non-SMEs, capped at $150,000 per enterprise
  • Nett fee model: you pay only your share
  • Roles mapped against the Jobs Transformation Maps
Explore WDG(JR+) →
Pillar 03

AI Transformation

Automate your workflows with 70% project funding, and train your team to run them.

  • Funded under WDG(JR+) Scope 3, paired with job redesign
  • Built and deployed by our Technology Partner, with team training included
  • From quotations and invoices to rostering and site reports
