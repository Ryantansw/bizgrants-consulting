Pillar 02 · WDG(JR+) job redesign

Redesign jobs with 70% funding.

The SkillsFuture Workforce Development Grant (Job Redesign+), or WDG(JR+), funds 70% of a job redesign project for SMEs and 50% for non-SMEs. Once the redesign is done, staff who move into the redesigned roles can be invited to apply for CCP salary support.

01 · Key facts

What WDG(JR+) is, in four lines.

SFEC timing. Current SFEC balances lapse on 30 Nov 2026. A new tranche is available from 1 Dec 2026.

  • A new grant. The SkillsFuture Workforce Development Grant (Job Redesign+) launched in March 2026 under the Enterprise Workforce Transformation Package. It replaces the job redesign support previously offered under PSG-JR. You may see it called the WSG job redesign grant: Workforce Singapore (WSG) and SkillsFuture Singapore merged into the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA) in July 2026, and SWDA now runs WDG(JR+).
  • 70% for SMEs, 50% for non-SMEs. Capped at $150,000 per enterprise across three scopes.
  • Nett fee model. You pay only your 30% share. The grant is paid to the consultant on milestones, so you don’t pay in full and then wait to claim.
  • SFEC helps further. The SkillsFuture Enterprise Credit (SFEC) can offset 90% of your Scope 1 nett fee.
02 · Three scopes

Three scopes, capped at $150,000 in total.

Scope 1 · capped at $50,000

Workforce Consultancy

Our core service. We map each role against the Jobs Transformation Map, redesign the tasks and job scope, and write the new job descriptions and training plans.

Scope 2 · capped at $60,000

Capability Building

Builds manager and HR capability to support the redesigned roles.

Scope 3 · capped at $90,000

Workforce Tech Solutions

Technology that enables the redesign, such as AI and automation. It must be paired with Scope 1 or Scope 2.

03 · Who qualifies

Eligibility, and the timing rule.

  • Registered in Singapore, with at least 3 local employees.
  • Timing matters. You must not sign or pay for the consultancy before the grant application is submitted.
  • All funding is subject to eligibility and approval. We check your position in the discovery call before anything is submitted.
04 · Redesign, then salary support

Redesign the job, then apply for CCP salary support.

WDG(JR+) funds the redesign

Scope 1 pays for the job redesign itself: the role mapping, the redesigned job scopes and the training plans.

CCP funds the salary support

Once Scope 1 is complete, staff with at least 12 months’ service who move into the redesigned roles can be invited to apply for CCP salary support: 3 months at 70% or 90% of monthly salary, subject to eligibility and approval. Salary support is paid under CCP, not by WDG(JR+).

06 · How it runs

Six steps from discovery call to milestone claims.

  1. Free discovery call. We learn how the work is changing and which roles it touches.

  2. Eligibility check and scoping. We confirm eligibility and scope at least 3 roles.

  3. Grant application. Submitted before anything is signed or paid.

  4. Workforce consultancy. We map each role against the Jobs Transformation Map, redesign the tasks and job scope, and write the new job descriptions and training plans.

  5. Staff move into the redesigned roles. We prepare their CCP applications.

  6. Milestone claims. The grant is paid on milestones, and you pay only your share.

10 · Common questions

WDG(JR+) questions employers ask.

Is this the same as PSG-JR?

It replaces it. WDG(JR+) took over job redesign support from PSG-JR when it launched in March 2026.

Who qualifies?

Companies registered in Singapore with at least 3 local employees, subject to eligibility and approval.

What will it cost me?

Your 30% share. SFEC can offset 90% of the Scope 1 nett fee.

Does WDG(JR+) pay salary support?

No. Salary support comes from CCP. Once Scope 1 is complete, staff in the redesigned roles can be invited to apply for it, subject to eligibility and approval.

Can I sign the proposal first?

No. The application must be submitted before you sign or pay.

Which roles can be redesigned?

We scope this with you in the discovery call, based on how the work is changing and the relevant Jobs Transformation Map.
